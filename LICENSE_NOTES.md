# License notes — COMP5523 sneakers sample

Small (~20 image) domain-specific text-to-image retrieval sample for sneakers.
Core labels: **brand** + **colour** only (v1).

## Sources
All images are from **Wikimedia Commons**, each with a free license
(CC0, Public Domain, CC BY, or CC BY-SA). No face-search or personal-data sources.
Brand names visible in photos are trademarks of their respective owners;
this sample uses them only as visual/domain labels for academic retrieval research.
